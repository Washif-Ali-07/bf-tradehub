Who we are

Bigfoot Tradehub (Singapore) Pte Ltd

Bigfoot Tradehub is the food-trading business of Bigfoot Group in Singapore. We trade rice, nuts and spices, bringing overseas supply to local markets and onward to other markets.

Product, volume and delivery needs are agreed with our team, so every trade is shaped around the customer.

01

Import

Bring food items into Singapore with supply, documentation and receipt requirements considered together.

02

Re-export

Connect imported food items with onward overseas markets, coordinating the next stage of trade.

03

Local market sales

Supply food items to local markets, with product, volume and delivery agreed with the team.
